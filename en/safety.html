FaceParty — Safety & community rules

last updated: 7 October 2026

FaceParty is for adults only and we want everyone to feel safe on it. This page summarises our community rules, our child-protection standards and what you can do if something is wrong.

Community rules

  • No minors: FaceParty is strictly for people aged 18 and over. Any account suspected of belonging to a minor is suspended and then deleted.
  • Respect: no harassment, insults, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Authenticity: no fake profiles, impersonation, catfishing or bots. Your photos must show you.
  • Consent: never publish a person’s photo or private words without their agreement; no non-consensual intimate images.
  • No scams or spam: no requests for money, commercial solicitation, romance or financial fraud.
  • No sexually explicit or illegal content.

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

  • FaceParty has zero tolerance for any content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAM/CSAE), including solicitation, sexualisation or grooming of minors.
  • Content of this kind is removed immediately, the account is permanently banned, and the facts are reported to the competent authorities (in France: the PHAROS platform, internet-signalement.gouv.fr; internationally: NCMEC / partner organisations), with preservation of the material needed for the investigation.
  • Every piece of content and every profile can be reported from the app (the “…” menu of the profile or conversation) or from the reporting page of the website. Reports involving children are handled as a priority.
  • Child-safety contact point: report form at rbngjkhgk.github.io/faceparty-legal/report.html (choose “Safety”).
  • The minimum age is checked at sign-up (date of birth declaration, refusal under 18) and verified with every report; the stores (App Store, Google Play) are informed that the app is for adults only.

Reporting and blocking

  • Block: from the profile or the conversation. The blocked person can no longer contact you or see you. The list is in Settings › Blocked accounts.
  • Report: from the profile, the conversation, or Settings › Report a problem. Your report is confidential: the reported person does not learn that it was you.
  • Moderation: the team reviews every report. Possible sanctions: removal of content, warning, suspension, permanent ban. Three serious breaches lead to an automatic ban, with human appeal possible.
  • Contest a decision: write to us via the contact form, giving your account e-mail.

Tips for going out safely

  • First date: public place, your own transport arranged, tell a friend or relative.
  • Do not share sensitive information (address, workplace, bank details) with someone you do not know.
  • Never send money, even for a claimed emergency; be wary of profiles that quickly move you to another messaging app.
  • Watch your drink and only accept it from the bartender’s hand; stay with people you trust.
  • If you feel in danger: 17 (police), 112 (European emergency), 114 (SMS for deaf or hard-of-hearing people), 3919 (violence against women). To report illegal content online: internet-signalement.gouv.fr.
