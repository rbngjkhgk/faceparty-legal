FaceParty — Privacy Policy

Version 3.2 — last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy transparently describes how FaceParty (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data, and what your rights are. It applies to the FaceParty mobile app (member side) and FaceParty Pro (store side). It is drafted in accordance with Articles 12 to 14 of the GDPR.

1. Definitions

  • Personal data : Means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing : any operation on data (collection, recording, consultation, communication, deletion, etc.).
  • Data Controller : the entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing (see section 2).
  • Processor : a service provider who processes data on our behalf and on our instructions (Art. 28 GDPR).
  • K-anonymity : technique ensuring that published data does not make it possible to isolate a person within a group of at least K individuals (in our case, K = 35 for establishment statistics).

Data processed by us

3.1 Account and identity data

  • e-mail address; password (stored only as a hash, never in clear text);
  • date of birth (to check the minimum age of 18);
  • displayed first name or nickname.

3.2 Profile data (provided by you)

  • profile photos; description, tagline, interests;
  • gender, declared orientation, size, eye and hair color, region and country of origin, preferred beverage;
  • discovery preferences (age range, distance, physical criteria).

3.3 Activity data

  • evenings and events joined, left, graded; tickets purchased;
  • facelikes sent and received, facematches, "conclusive facematch" responses;
  • messages exchanged in conversations and in reporting threads;
  • friends added, friend requests, friend code, private events created or joined;
  • reports and blocks you make.

3.4 Location data

  • approximate position of the device, only when the app is open and with your system permission, to list places around you and calculate distances. Revocable at any time in the phone settings; the app remains usable by entering a city manually.

3.5 Technical data

  • account identifier, language; push-notification token if you accept it; IP address and timestamp of your connections (technical and security logs of the host). No third-party audience-measurement or crash-reporting tool is used.

3.6 Payment Data

If purchases (FaceParty + subscription, tickets) are offered, they are handled by Apple’s App Store, Google Play and/or our ticketing provider. We receive a transaction confirmation and a purchase identifier, but never your card number.

3.7 “Sensitive” Data

We do not solicit sensitive data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. Certain information you choose to publish (origin, orientation) may be relevant: you provide it freely, on the basis of your explicit consent (art. 9.2.a), and may withdraw it at any time from your profile, without affecting access to the service.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Create / manage your account and provide the service (profiles, facelikes, matches, messaging, friends, events) — contract performance (art. 6.1.b).
  • Check the minimum age of 18 years — legal obligation and legitimate interest to protect minors (art. 6.1.c / 6.1.f).
  • Show Nearby Places & Distances — consent (art. 6.1.a) for geolocation.
  • Security, prevention and treatment of fraud and abuse, moderation, management of reports and blockages — legitimate interest and as appropriate to legal obligation (art. 6.1.f / 6.1.c).
  • Push notifications consent (art. 6.1.a), revocable in the settings.
  • FaceParty AI (premium recommendations) — contract performance for subscribers; local calculation, not mandatory (see section 9).
  • Anonymised statistics for establishments — legitimate interest ; aggregated and k-anonymised data, with no possibility of re-identification (see section 8).
  • Subscription and ticket management, accounting and tax obligations — contract performance and legal obligation.

When processing is based on legitimate interest, we have carried out a balancing exercise with your rights and freedoms; you can ask us for a summary and oppose it (section 10).

5. Recipients and subcontractors

Your data is neither sold, rented nor exchanged for advertising purposes. It is accessible only to the publisher’s authorised teams and to the following processors, acting on written instructions and bound by a processing agreement (GDPR art. 28):

  • Hosting, database, storage: Supabase (database, authentication, storage, functions), infrastructure hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt) — role: infrastructure.
  • Push notifications: Expo’s notification service, then Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) — role: technical delivery; they receive your device token and the content of the notification.
  • Payment and invoicing: Apple (App Store), Google (Play Store); [ticketing provider] — role: collection, receipts.
  • Audio / video calls: they are set up directly between the two phones (peer-to-peer, encrypted). To connect them, technical STUN / TURN servers (Google, Open Relay) see the devices’ IP address, never the content of the call.
  • Publisher’s moderation team — role: handling reports. A venue where an incident occurred never receives never your identity; he exchanges with you anonymously. Only if the institution confirms a report as serious and well-founded that the file is sent to the publisher's moderation team, this time with the identity of the person person concerned (never that of the declarant), for the safety of members (art. 6.1.f — overriding legitimate interest in preventing violence; art. 9.2.g for serious events).
  • Address geocoding: OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — role: converting an entered address into coordinates; only the entered address is sent, never your identity.
  • AI assistance for venues (reading drink menus and playlists): Anthropic — receives only the photos submitted by the venue, never member data.

The up-to-date named list of processors is available on request via the contact form.

Other members only see your public profile (first name, age, photos, description, interests, declared criteria) and the messages you send them. Institutions only receive anonymized statistics (Section 8).

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Our servers are located in the European Economic Area (EEA). Some providers (push notifications, calls, AI assistance, the host’s publisher) may process data outside the EEA, notably in the United States; the transfer is then governed by: (i) a European Commission adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework), or (ii) the Commission’s standard contractual clauses (decision 2021/914), supplemented where necessary by additional technical and organisational measures (encryption, pseudonymisation, transfer impact assessment).

Retention timeline

  • Active account: for the duration of use of the service.
  • After deletion of the account: deletion of profile and content data within 30 days; purge of encrypted backups within a maximum of 90 days.
  • Conversation messages: deleted with the account or with the conversation.
  • Friend request not accepted: Linked conversation and facematch are deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • Reporting threads: retained for processing time, then up to 12 months after closure (or longer if required by a procedure).
  • Serious reports confirmed by a venue and sent to the publisher, including the identity of the person concerned: up to [24] months after closure, for the purpose of preventing repeat offences and as evidence in the event of a dispute or proceedings.
  • Audit log of moderation actions (Art. 5.2 GDPR — liability): up to [36] months.
  • Bans: for their entire duration, then [24] months after their eventual lifting.
  • Security and login logs: up to 12 months.
  • Moderation items (reports, blocks): up to 12 months after closure.
  • Accounting documents (purchases, tickets, invoices): 10 years (legal obligation — Article L. 123-22 of the Commercial Code).
  • Consents (geolocation, notifications): trace kept 3 years after their withdrawal, for evidentiary purposes.

8. Establishment statistics & anonymization

FaceParty Pro provides institutions with dashboards exclusively aggregated and anonymised : composition of the clientele, tastes, frequency of exit, loyalty, cross-origin, etc.

  • No identifier, name, photo, message or contact data of a member is transmitted to an establishment.
  • Any value or category with fewer than 35 people is removed and displayed as "< 35". Some measurements are also rounded and/or noisy.
  • The crossings used to re-identify a person are technically blocked (removal of too fine cells, absence of free filters on individual data).
  • The establishments are bound by contract (FaceParty Pro conditions + processing agreement) not to attempt re-identification and to use limited to the knowledge of their public.

These data, once anonymized, no longer constitute personal data within the meaning of the GDPR; they nevertheless remain subject to the above guarantees.

Automated decision-making (profiling)

The "FaceParty AI" functions (places where your profile pleases, compatibility ranking and probability of "like in return", suggestions) produce calculated recommendations locally on your phone based on your past facelikes and facematches. These calculations never send your data to an external service.

This is a sorting aid. These recommendations have no legal effect and do not significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). You remain free to faceliker whoever you want, to ignore these suggestions and not to use these screens.

Automatic ban for safety. Only one decision is likely to be taken in an automated way: when a member is subject to three separate reports qualified as “serious and well-founded” by the venues concerned, their account is automatically suspended. This measure is necessary for members’ safety and for the performance of our terms of use (art. 22.2.a and 22.2.b GDPR). You are informed of the decision and its reason; you can contest it, express your point of view and obtain a review by a human being through the contact form (rbngjkhgk.github.io/faceparty-legal/contact.html). The publisher may also lift, or conversely impose, a ban before this threshold after human review of a case.

We do not carry out any profiling for advertising purposes and do not practice any behavioural advertising.

10. Your rights

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR and the Data Protection Act, you have the following rights:

  • Access : obtain confirmation that processing is taking place and a copy of your data.
  • Rectification : correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion (“right to be forgotten”): request the deletion of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction : freeze the use of certain data during a verification.
  • Portability : receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Objection : object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawing your consent : at any time, without retroactive effect (geolocation, notifications, data you have chosen to publish).
  • Post-mortem guidelines : define the fate of your data after your death.

How to exercise your rights

  • From the app: Settings › Download my data (access and portability: a readable file with all your data); Profile › Edit profile (rectification); Settings › Delete my account (immediate erasure of the account and your data).
  • Via the contact form: rbngjkhgk.github.io/faceparty-legal/contact.html. We reply within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests (we will tell you). An ID document may be requested if there is reasonable doubt about your identity, and is then kept only for the time of verification.

11. Moderation & safety of people

You can report or block a member at any time. When a report is made during an evening, the establishment concerned is alerted immediately and can communicate with you via a dedicated thread to intervene. In this exchange, the venue sees neither your identity, nor your photo, nor your profile : only the pattern and your messages are sent to it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and may result in a warning, suspension or deletion of the account in question.

12. Safety

  • Hashed passwords (SEL algorithm); Encryption of Transit Exchanges (TLS) and Resting Backups;
  • internal access control according to the principle of least privilege, access logging;
  • minimization and pseudonymization of data as much as possible;
  • regular safety tests and review of subcontractors;
  • incident management procedures.

Data breach

In the event of a data breach that may result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the CNIL within 72 hours and inform you as soon as possible when the risk is high, in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

14. Impact Assessment (IAA)

Given the nature of the service (matchmaking, location data, data that may reveal orientation or origin), a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is maintained and reassessed at each major evolution of the product.

15. Minors

FaceParty is strictly reserved for people aged 18 or over. The date of birth is checked at sign-up (refusal under 18). Any account identified as belonging to a minor is deleted without delay. Report such an account via “Report” or via the contact form.

16. Cookies, SDKs and trackers

The app does not use advertising cookies or third-party targeting trackers, and uses no advertising identifier (IDFA). Only the following are used: local technical storage needed for operation (session, preferences) and the notification service if you accept it. No third-party audience measurement is used.

17. Claim

If you believe the processing of your data is non-compliant, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07 — www.cnil.fr) or with the supervisory authority of your EU country of residence. We invite you, however, to contact us first so we can find a quick solution.

Changes to the Policy

This policy may change. In the event of a significant change, you will be informed in the application or by email before the entry into force. The version and date of last update are at the top of the document; version history is available upon request.
